# Claim ledger

Every substantive claim must have one status:

- **Established:** directly supported by mature theory or authoritative evidence
- **Adapted:** established concept applied in a new context
- **Proposed:** operational definition or hypothesis introduced here
- **Speculative:** interesting but presently weak or untestable
- **Falsified:** contradicted by evidence or internal analysis
- **Unresolved:** status awaits research
