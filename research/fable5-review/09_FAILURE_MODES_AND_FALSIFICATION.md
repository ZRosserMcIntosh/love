# 09 — Failure modes and falsification

## A. Thirty-two hostile counterexamples

Verdict key: **H** = framework handles it as-is · **V** = handles it only with an
additional variable (named) · **N** = target quantity becomes non-identifiable
(bounds only) · **F** = framework fails / must refuse.

| # | Case | Verdict | Analysis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stalker expending enormous energy | **V** | Energetics look identical to devotion — high \(E\), high persistence. Separation requires the signature variables (02 §Q5): benefit-insensitivity, escalation on refusal, consent = absent. Consent gate excludes it from any love-attributed aggregate. A framework without the gate **fails** this case; with it, handled. |
| 2 | Coercive partner's grand gestures | **V** | Same as #1 plus recipient-fear covariates. Gestures with recipient-reported coercion are recorded, never celebrated; motive complex codes to fear/control. The energetics alone *cannot* see this — the honesty is in admitting that. |
| 3 | Parent quietly remaining beside a child | **H** | Near-zero incremental energy, high commitment. Correctly reported: \(\Delta\approx0\), attribution high, benefit real. Proves the framework does not equate love with joules — the *point* of A6/A7 axioms. |
| 4 | Efficient act that saves energy | **H** | Negative induced energy is native (08 §7). |

| 8 | Disabled actor with metabolically expensive movement | **V** | Raw joules discriminate; within-person baseline normalization (05 §6) is the only fair frame. Cross-person energy comparisons are prohibited anyway. |

## B. The falsification mandate (§XIX), answered

1. **What would falsify each central claim?**
   - *Recipient-dependence of energetic cost tolerance (C-010/H1):* two adequately
     powered preregistered nulls in S1 → falsified (Gate G1).
   - *Allocated/induced distinctness (C-006/007/H4):* scenario contrasts
     indistinguishable across realistic projects in S3 → the distinction is idle.
   - *Attribution estimability (C-008/H3):* prospective reports, incentive-margin
     estimates, and panel codings failing convergent correlation (preregistered
     threshold) in S3 → attribution layer dies; only scenario contrasts survive.
   - *Incremental validity (C-011/H2):* ΔAUC ≤ 0 in S5's confirmatory season →
     joule denomination is decorative (Gate G2).
2. **What would show the quantities lack reliability?** Test–retest ICC < 0.6 on
   ledger totals under unchanged regimes (S3 repeat windows); wearable-dependent
   sign flips in \(\Delta(s)\); panel attribution intervals failing inter-panel
   reproducibility.
3. **Lack of construct validity?** Energetic measures loading on general
   activity/conscientiousness rather than relationship-specific factors — the S5
   negative-control outcome (job change) predicting as well as commitment outcomes
   is the tell.
4. **No predictive value beyond existing measures?** Precisely G2; also S2's
   cross-currency correlation ρ→1 (energy discounting fully explained by money
   discounting) would show the energy frame adds nothing even in the lab.
5. **Terminological repackaging?** If, after G1–G4, every surviving result could be
   published in existing venues under existing names with zero loss — the program
   should take that outcome gracefully: merge, and leave behind the protocol.
6. **Permanently non-identifiable attribution?** If G4 simulations show PN bounds
   ≈ [0,1] under *every* feasible design including lab-calibrated field hybrids —
   then drop attribution from the field program forever; scenario contrasts only.
7. **What survives with "love" replaced by any motive?** Everything mathematical
   (00 F3). The love-specific surplus is: the construct ontology (02), the consent/
   coercion ethics layer, the relationship-category estimands, and the application
   value. Hence the rename: **causal motivational energetics** as the general field;
   Agapodynamics as its love-and-care application. This is a conclusion, not a
   concession.

## C. Failure-condition synthesis (extends `docs/MEASUREMENT_AND_FALSIFIABILITY.md`)

The program should be rejected, renamed, or radically narrowed if any of: G1–G4
fail as specified (00); the pseudo-precision guard (05 §7) cannot be satisfied by
any honest pipeline; the consent/harm architecture proves unenforceable in real
deployments (#31 materializing at scale); or a systematic review surfaces a
predecessor field already doing S1–S3 under another name — in which case cite,
merge, and contribute the protocol rather than the brand.
