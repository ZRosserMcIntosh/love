# Agent instructions

These rules govern all AI-assisted work in this repository.

1. Never describe love as a physical energy or force.
2. Use SI units internally. State every non-SI conversion explicitly.
3. Keep allocated energy separate from induced energy.
4. State the counterfactual and system boundary for every numerical result.
5. Never add a child energy flow to a parent flow that already contains it.
6. Treat causal attribution as an uncertain estimate, not observed fact.
7. Do not convert money, time, grief, risk, consent, or moral worth into joules
   without a separately defended model. Prefer a multidimensional result.
8. Do not infer the depth or goodness of love from expenditure alone.
9. Distinguish descriptive physical quantities from normative benefit or harm.
10. Use synthetic data in the public repository.
11. Never invent citations. Prefer primary literature and authoritative
    measurement standards; record DOI or stable source URLs.
12. Add a dimensional-analysis explanation and at least one test whenever a
    mathematical function is added.
13. Mark every substantive proposition as established, adapted, inferred,
    proposed, speculative, or falsified in the claim ledger.
14. Preserve consent, recipient welfare, and non-coercion as independent
    ethical variables. Energetic magnitude never overrides them.

The default posture is constructive skepticism: build the strongest possible
framework, then try to destroy it.
