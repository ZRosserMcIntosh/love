# Authoritative starting sources

This is a seed list, not a systematic review.

## Units and energy equivalence

- National Institute of Standards and Technology, Guide to the SI, Appendix
  B.8. Includes the conventional 4.184 × 10^9 J per ton TNT conversion.
  https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors/nist-guide-si-appendix-b8

## Human energy expenditure

- International Atomic Energy Agency guidance on the doubly labelled water
  method for total daily energy expenditure.
  https://www.iaea.org/resources/hhc/nutrition/total-energy-expenditure/guidance-material

- Herrmann SD et al. 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities: A third
  update of the energy costs of human activities. Journal of Sport and Health
  Science. PMID 38242596.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38242596/

## Brain energy

- Raichle ME, Gusnard DA. Appraising the brain's energy budget. Proceedings of
  the National Academy of Sciences. 2002;99(16):10237–10239.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC124895/

- Attwell D, Laughlin SB. An energy budget for signaling in the grey matter of
  the brain. Journal of Cerebral Blood Flow and Metabolism.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11598490/

## Causal inference

- Hernán MA, Robins JM. Causal Inference: What If. Chapman & Hall/CRC. Free
  author-hosted text and resources:
  https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/causal-inference-book/

## Costly helping

- Raihani NJ, Smith S. Competitive helping in online giving. Current Biology.
  2015;25(9):1183–1186.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25891407/

- Bhogal MS, Galbraith N, Manktelow K. Physical attractiveness and altruism in
  two modified dictator games. Basic and Applied Social Psychology.
  https://doi.org/10.1080/01973533.2016.1216651

## Evidence policy

Every future source entry should include:

- exact claim supported;
- source type;
- DOI or stable URL;
- population and method;
- major limitations;
- whether the repository relies on the source as established fact,
  approximation, analogy, or disputed evidence.
